Measures to ensure the well-being of both, our Captions and Customers.

Insurance

Insurance can be claimed for any accident that occurs during the ride covering OPD treatment, hospitalisation, and accidental benefit with a maximum sum insured of $5 Lakh. it can be claimed as soon as the ride

24 x 7 Customer Support

Both, our captions and customers can report any kind of issues to Rapido through the 24*7 support feature on the app post & during the ride

Two-Way Rating System

Post the ride, both parties can give a rating to each other and any rating below 3 is flagged from Rapido's end. Rapido reaches out to them in 10 minutes to address their concern.

Way forward on Safety

To further substantiate our commitment to Safety First towards Customers & captains, we will be incorporating new features on our platform very soon.
